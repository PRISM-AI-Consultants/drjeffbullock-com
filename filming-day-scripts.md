# Filming Day — Dr. Jeff Bullock

**Setup:** Black PRISM polo. Phone HORIZONTAL, at eye level (prop it up). Face a window for light. Look INTO the lens. 60 to 90 seconds each. Riff off the beats, the words are just a guide. A real stumble beats a perfect take.

**Order to shoot:** Start with #1 (you know it cold), then #2 and #3 (the fire), end with #4. Re-prop and go.

## 1. THE HOOK — Two Dreams (homepage hero, ~60s)

Beats: who you are, drop credentials fast → Dream 1: giving viral → Dream 2: end poverty, start home → everything points at these two → come be in it.

> "Hey. I'm Jeff Bullock. People know me as a pharmacist. These days I build with AI. But that's not the point. The point is two dreams I can't put down.
>
> First one: I want to make giving go viral. Facebook and Instagram built the most powerful habit in human history and pointed it at the scroll. I want to point that same machine at generosity. Giving that feels as good, and spreads as fast, as the feed.
>
> Second one's bigger. I want to end poverty. For everyone. I'm starting in my own community, because that's where I have the credibility to lead, and because we can flip generational wealth in one generation instead of three. Prove it there, it spreads everywhere.
>
> Every company I run, every book, every bit of research points at those two things. Build the money first, so we actually have something to give. Then give it away, and watch what happens.
>
> That's the work. I'd love for you to be in it with me."

---

## 2. THE REFRAME — Giving Is Selfish (~80s, signature piece)

Beats: provocation (most selfish thing is to give) → why: you get more than you give up → the trifecta vs cheap dopamine → the other wins (health, people like you more, meaning) → the trick: give what you want, where you want.

> "Let me tell you the most selfish thing you can do. Give.
>
> I know how that sounds. Stay with me. When you give, your brain doesn't just hand you a quick hit of dopamine like the scroll does. It gives you the whole cocktail. Dopamine, serotonin, oxytocin. The kind of good that lasts, instead of the kind that crashes and leaves you needing more.
>
> It lowers your stress. It helps you sleep. People literally like you more. You feel like your life means something. You get all of that, and all you had to do was give a little.
>
> You come out ahead. Every time.
>
> And here's the trick nobody tells you. Give what you want to give. Don't make it a chore. Give where you want, to what moves you. That's the power. You get to choose. That is the whole thing I'm building."

---

## 3. THE ABUNDANCE — Poverty Is a Construct (~80s)

Beats: abundance mindset, abundant times → poverty is man-made, so we can un-make it → the money exists (AI trillions), it costs less than you think → I'm not waiting on anybody, start with yourself → my community, Black Advantage, build systems so others can too.

> "I have an abundance mindset, because we live in abundant times. Look around. Look at the trillions moving through AI right now.
>
> Poverty is not a law of nature. It is man made. It's a construct. And anything we built, we can take apart.
>
> When you actually run the math on what it would cost to lift people out of poverty, it is smaller than you think. The money is already here. It is just pointed at the wrong things.
>
> So I'm not waiting on a government or a billionaire to fix it. I'm starting with myself, and with my own community. That's what The Black Advantage is about. And I'm building systems so anybody who sees what I see can do the same thing, themselves.
>
> One person. Then a community. Then everybody."

---

## 4. THE FOUNDATION — Being Your Best Self (~80s)

Beats: why a pharmacist studies performance → can't give or build from an empty tank, unlock the person first → the work: Four Agreements, master your own mind and habits → AI amplifies whoever you already are → best self, build, end poverty, give: one thing from four sides.

> "People ask why a pharmacist is over here studying psychedelics, learning science, human performance. Here's why.
>
> You can't give from an empty tank. You can't build from one either. So before any of it, you have to unlock the person. You.
>
> That's the work I do on myself. Living by something like the Four Agreements. Mastering my own mind and my own habits before I try to master anything out there.
>
> And AI is the tool that changes the math. It lets one person build like a whole company. It amplifies whoever you already are, so become someone worth amplifying.
>
> Best self, build wealth, end poverty, give it away. For me, those aren't four different things. They're one thing, seen from four sides."
