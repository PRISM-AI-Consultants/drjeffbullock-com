Fair Capital is a free education tool for small-business owners. It shows what a merchant cash advance or loan really costs before you sign, checks your capital readiness, and points you to fair capital in the Lehigh Valley.

## The AI Capital Agent

Upload your bank statements and a cash advance contract. The agent reads them and shows every advance and its real cost.

- [Fair Capital home](https://faircapital.prismaiconsultants.com/)
- [The AI Capital Agent](https://faircapital.prismaiconsultants.com/agent.html)

Fair Capital is education only. It is not a lender and does not take money from lenders.
