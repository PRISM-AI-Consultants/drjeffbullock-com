Back Lot is a match-3 puzzle game about bringing an old television studio back to life. Dr. Jeff Bullock made it for his mother, who loves match-3 games where you build something as you play. It is built for a phone, with big pieces and nothing to rush.

## How It Plays

- Swap two pieces to make a row of three: lumber, paint, bulbs, hammers, fabric, plants and pillows
- Every piece you clear on a board goes in your supply shed
- Win the board, then spend the supplies on the next job on the studio lot

## Rebuild the Studio

Each sitcom set comes back in five steps you can see: draw the plans, raise the walls, paint, move in the furniture and turn on the lights.

Every day there is a new Overtime board. No timers and no ads.
