Handed Down is a tabletop VR game from PRISM Studios, created by Dr. Jeff Bullock, for the Meta Quest browser. A storm rolls over a tiny island village and you shelter a villager named Mira with your own hand. Later, when a child is caught in the rain, Mira remembers what you did and holds a leaf over him.

## How It Plays

- The island village sits on a table in front of you
- Reach in and shelter a villager from the storm with your own hand
- The ways you help the villagers become skills they pass to each other

## Play It

Open it in the Meta Quest browser. It runs best in a VR headset. [Play Handed Down](https://vr.prismaiconsultants.com/handed-down/).
