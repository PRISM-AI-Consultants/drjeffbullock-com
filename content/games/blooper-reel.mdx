Blooper Reel is a spot-the-difference game set on painted sitcom sets. Dr. Jeff Bullock made it for his mother, who loves sitcoms and spot-the-difference puzzles. It is built for a phone, with big pictures and nothing to rush.

## How It Plays

- Every scene opens with a clapperboard, then shows two takes of the same set
- Something changed between the takes. Tap what changed to catch each blooper
- The sets are classic sitcom rooms: a living room, a diner, a family kitchen, a coffee shop, a barbershop and more
- There is no timer, so you can look as long as you like

## The Studio Audience

The audience cheers every blooper you catch. Stuck on one? Ask the audience for a hint.

## That's a Wrap

Finish a scene and you get a wrap card with a clean, original joke. Then pick the next scene from the call sheet.
