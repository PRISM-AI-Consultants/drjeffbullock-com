But If Not is the first game in Heroes vs Villains, a collection of Bible stories you play to the song. It is built on Daniel chapter 3 in the King James Version and on Dr. Jeff Bullock's song "But If Not."

## How It Plays

- Waves of BOW roll out from the golden image, the king's throne and the fiery furnace toward Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego
- Tap the brother each wave is about to hit, right as it lands, on the beat of the song
- Hold on every "but if not" and the three link arms
- The furnace is heated seven times hotter, and then the fourth man appears in the fire
- A two-minute story mode, the full song, and a one-button mode for young children

## The Story

The brothers never bow, because Scripture says they did not. Your timing decides how steady they stand. Eleven King James verses appear as the story unfolds, ending on Daniel 3:18: "But if not, be it known unto thee, O king, that we will not serve thy gods, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up."
