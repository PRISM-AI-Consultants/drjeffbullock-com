One More Load is a real-time dispatch game built from a real two-truck carrier in Allentown, PA. You can be great at dispatching and still own a terrible business.

## Features

- Five days across 2022 and 2023, with real spot rates and the record diesel price
- Book loads off a live corridor map, counter the broker, and pre-book a truck's next load
- Driver call-offs, breakdowns, detention, lumpers, double brokers and a law firm letter
- Two doors: buy the trucks, or start a dispatch service and keep 8%
- An end-of-day ledger that shows the gross, the net, and what the bank actually did
