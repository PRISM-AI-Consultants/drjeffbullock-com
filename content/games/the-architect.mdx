The Architect is a survivors-style arcade game starring Dr. Jeff. The city has gone gray under the Inertia. You fight back by drawing a gold blueprint line behind you as you move.

## How It Plays

- Close your gold line into a loop and everyone inside it is freed
- Each freed block turns from gray ink back to full watercolor color
- Powers drawn from the six domains of Far Transfer
- Original Dr. Jeff soundtrack

Free in your browser, on desktop or phone. [Play The Architect on PRISM Studios](https://prismstudios.app/the-architect/).
