Fruit of the Spirit is a match-3 puzzle game built on Galatians 5:22-23: "But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, temperance: against such there is no law." Dr. Jeff Bullock made it first for his mother, who loves puzzle games on her phone.

## How It Plays

- Swap two fruits to make a row of three. Love is a strawberry, joy an orange, peace an olive, and so on through all nine
- Line up four for a Sunbeam, make an L or T for a Blossom, and line up five for a Dove that gathers every fruit of one kind
- Clear the thorns from the Parable of the Sower and break up the stony ground
- 45 levels across nine painted gardens, one for each fruit of the Spirit

## Made for Every Morning

Every win gives you a King James verse about that fruit, and your verses are saved in your own collection. A new Daily Harvest puzzle and verse arrives every day. Big pieces, no timers, no lives and no ads, with an original gospel soul-jazz soundtrack.
