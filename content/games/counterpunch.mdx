Counterpunch is a 60-second arcade boxing game. Read the tell, slip the punch, and make your opponent pay for every opening.

## Features

- Dodge and punish: head shots and body shots, timed off your opponent's openings
- Six champions to work through
- Dr. Jeff Rx picks between rounds
- Leaderboard, album, and a training mode
- Challenge links so you can send a friend the exact fight you just won
- Original soundtrack

## Play It

Free in your browser, on desktop or phone. [Play Counterpunch on PRISM Studios](https://prismstudios.app/counterpunch/).
