Bash the Busywork is a fast survivors-style arcade game about the work that quietly eats your week. Emails, approvals, status pings, and endless little tasks swarm in from every side. Your weapons are AI agents, and your job is to deploy them fast enough to keep the busywork from burying you.

It plays on your phone, tracks a live leaderboard, and turns a very real frustration into something you can actually beat.

## Why This Game Exists

Everyone loses hours to work that does not need a human. This game makes the point in the most direct way possible: point automation at the busywork and watch it clear.
