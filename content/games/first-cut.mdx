First Cut puts you inside the shoes of a surgical resident. Ten shifts of escalating chaos, competing demands, and split-second calls. Triage the floor, keep your patients stable, and survive the schedule without burning out.

It is a love letter to everyone who has worked a shift that never seemed to end, built in the same authentic spirit as Pharmageddon.

## The Real Story

Like every PRISM Studios sim, First Cut is grounded in the real texture of the work it depicts. The pressure is the point.
