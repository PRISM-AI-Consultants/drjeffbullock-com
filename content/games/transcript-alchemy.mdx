Transcript Alchemy: Gold Rush turns the book's core skill into a game. A conversation scrolls by. You mine the phrases that matter, fuse them into something new, and sell what you make. Every gold creation is a real play from [Transcript Alchemy](/books/transcript-alchemy), like the Voice of Customer-to-Ad Copy Pipeline (#3) and the Time-Capsule Letter to the Future (#101).

## Features

- Eight stories across the book's four domains: work, self, craft and legacy
- 29 discoveries to find by fusing and merging, each tied to a play from the book
- Glim, the AI notetaker: some notes are true, some are invented, and you have to check the line each one cites
- Legacy stories end with a keepsake card of the real words you saved, ready to download
- A Daily Rush with a shareable score, plus a no-clock Laboratory mode
- An original soundtrack
