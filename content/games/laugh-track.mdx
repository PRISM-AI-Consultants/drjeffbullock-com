Laugh Track is a match-3 puzzle game set on a night of television. Dr. Jeff Bullock made it for his mother, who loves sitcoms, movies and anything funny. It is built for a phone, with big pieces and nothing to rush.

## How It Plays

- Swap two pieces to make a row of three: popcorn, TV sets, sodas, clapperboards, tickets, donuts and couches
- Line up four for a Spotlight, make an L or T for Star Power, and line up five for the Clicker, which clears every piece of one kind
- Clear the TV static and the VHS tapes that get in the way
- The game is laid out in seasons that start in the Seventies, and every level is an episode with its own sitcom-style title

## The Studio Audience

The audience reacts to how you play. A short chain gets a chuckle. Keep it going and you get a laugh, then a big laugh, then applause. Run out of moves and they give you a kind "aww" before you try again.

## Name That Show

Win an episode and you get one Name That Show question about a classic sitcom, with three choices. A wrong pick just fades away, so you always land on the answer, and every show you name goes on your My Shows shelf. Each question is checked against its source before it goes in the game.

Every day there is a new Movie Night board with a movie question and a clean joke. No timers, no lives and no ads, with an original soundtrack that changes with the decade.
