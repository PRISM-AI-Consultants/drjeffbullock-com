Potluck is a feel-good match-3 puzzle game about a family potluck, where everybody brings a dish. Dr. Jeff Bullock made it for his mother, who loves farm-style match-3 games. It is built for a phone, with big pieces and nothing to rush.

## How It Plays

- Swap two pieces to make a row of three: tomatoes, corn, carrots, greens, eggplant, blueberries and eggs
- Gather what each dish needs to get it on the table
- A match makes the pieces right next to it worth more, so where you match matters

## Wilted Pieces and Hungry Guests

Some pieces wilt and are worth nothing until you freshen them up. Now and then a hungry guest shows up with an empty plate, and every piece you gather counts toward feeding them.

Every day there is a new Leftovers board. No timers and no ads.
