The Leash is a co-op action game from the world of Symbiont: Dyad, the first book in the Dyad Cycle by Dr. Jeff Bullock. Two strangers. One bond. Drift too far and you both die.

## How It Plays

- Your left thumb moves Kofi, your right thumb moves Rae
- Fight close together to fill the Charge, then snap together when it hits
- Let them drift too far apart and you both bleed
- Songs from the One Shadow album
