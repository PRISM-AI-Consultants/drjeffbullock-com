Claire Beaumont, host of the true-crime podcast Sins of the South, arrives at her family's decaying Mississippi plantation to film Season Three. The house turns on her fast: a phantom lullaby, a portrait that watches, and the ghost of Esther Morrow, a midwife her great-grandmother Evelyn destroyed with a lie in 1953. Claire learns Evelyn's cruelty condemned seven people, the "Creek Seven," and that an older curse, laid by an enslaved conjure woman named Amina in 1863 against the plantation's overseer, has fed on Beaumont lies ever since, resurfacing on a thirty-year cycle. Local mechanic Jonah Carter, guardian of his niece Zora and keeper of a hidden archive of Black history, reluctantly allies with Claire once the entity, the Hunger, starts endangering the town.

Working from Claire's late mother's unfinished research, the two descend into the plantation's cellar to broadcast the Seven's true stories from the root of the ancient oak anchoring the curse. Mid-broadcast the Hunger seizes Claire's voice, speaking Evelyn's old lies through her and offering a vision of fame if she frames Jonah for the fire that's coming. Refusing to let the lie leave her mouth, Claire bites off her own tongue. The sacrifice breaks the curse; Jonah burns the root and the house collapses.

The Seven's spirits are released. Left permanently mute, Claire recovers at Jonah's home while the broadcast goes viral, some treating it as a hoax, others using it to recover the Seven's real histories. She leaves for Charleston having finally told a story she can never take back and never own.

## Themes

- The ethics of trauma tourism and turning Black suffering into true-crime content
- Inherited guilt and reckoning with wealth built on racial violence
- The difference between telling a truth and owning it for profit
- Erasure versus preservation of silenced Black history
- Sacrifice as the only offering a system of exploitation cannot consume

## Characters

- Claire Beaumont: ambitious true-crime podcaster who discovers her empathy and her exploitation run on the same instinct
- Jonah Carter: mechanic and secret archivist protecting his niece and his community's buried history
- Evelyn Beaumont: Claire's great-grandmother, whose calculated 1953 lies destroyed seven lives and still speaks through the house
- The Creek Seven (Esther Morrow, Thomas 'Red' Carter, Pastor Solomon Price, Caleb Jenkins, Ruth, Missy and Marlon Hill, Elijah Franks): the seven people Evelyn destroyed, now trapped as spirits
- Ada Carter and Zora: the child witness of 1953 and the psychically sensitive niece decades later, both marked by the curse
- Amina: the enslaved conjure woman whose 1863 curse against a lying overseer became the entity called the Hunger

## Who It's For

For readers of Southern Gothic horror who want the haunted house to carry real historical weight. Sells on a hook true-crime fans will recognize (a podcaster whose own content becomes the horror) paired with body horror and a genuine reckoning with racial violence, not just atmosphere.
