The kind of business advisor that used to cost more than a small shop makes is now in your pocket, for the price of a phone you already carry. And it works in Spanish.

El Asesor que Sí Puedes Pagar is a short, story-driven book, written in Spanish, that teaches small-business owners how to turn everyday AI into a real business partner, using only the free app on the phone they already own. You meet Rosa, a Colombian baker with a line out the door and a stack of English paperwork no one ever explained to her. She hands the hardest thing on her counter to the tool, gets back plain Spanish and a clear next step, and handles it herself. That is the whole spirit of the book: the owner is the one who wins, not the coach.

A note on how it was made. Dr. Jeff Bullock is not Latino, and he does not pretend to speak for an experience that is not his. He is the coach who built the method across hundreds of sessions, and the book was written in Spanish with its voice tuned by Colombian collaborators, so it reads like your block and not a translation.

## What is inside

- The scary English letter, understood and answered in your own language
- La Receta, a simple recipe for talking to AI so it works on your business
- The money plays: decode a loan denial, price with real margin, market on five dollars a day
- The honest limits, so you always stay in charge
- Copy-paste prompts in Spanish you can run tonight

## Who this is for

Owners who were never handed the manual, because the tools showed up in English and the people who knew stayed quiet. The panadería and the food truck, yes, and the contractor, the salón, the clinic, the accountant, the shop that has been in the family twenty years.
