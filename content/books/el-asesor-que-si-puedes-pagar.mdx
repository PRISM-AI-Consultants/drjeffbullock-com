The most valuable business advisor you could ever hire is already in your pocket. It works in Spanish. It never sleeps. And it costs almost nothing.

El Asesor que Sí Puedes Pagar, "The Advisor You Can Finally Afford," is a short, story-driven book that teaches Latino small-business owners how to turn everyday AI into a real business partner, using only the free app on the phone they already own. Through Rosa, a Colombian panadera drowning in paperwork she cannot read, it shows the move that changes everything: hand the hardest thing on your counter to the tool, and get back plain Spanish, a clear next step, and your evenings.

Written natively in Spanish and rooted in what Dr. Jeff Bullock actually teaches across hundreds of coaching sessions.

## What is inside

- The scary English letter, understood and answered in your own language
- La Receta, the five-ingredient recipe for talking to AI so it works on your business
- The money plays: decode a loan denial, price with real margin, a month of marketing on five dollars a day
- The honest limits, so you always stay in charge
- Copy-paste prompts in Spanish you can run tonight

## Who this is for

Small-business owners of Latin descent who were told AI was not for them. The taquería, the salón, the panadería, the food truck. If you have ever felt the language was a tax you keep paying, this book hands you the tool that finally works for you.
